**[07:44]** Cheap and cheerful **Bell's Blended Scotch Whisky** for chest warmers while setting up camp down in the shooting pit on Cold Law. I haven't had a Bell's for a while, and because it's so freezing in this hole in the ground, that's not too bad!

**[38:56]** Now for **Brewmeister Snake Venom (67.5% ABV)** — *"The World's Strongest Beer"*, sent in by Johnny M:
* **Nose:** Smells absolutely disgusting!
* **Taste:** Ah, like **charcoal and vinegar**! That's horrible — Johnny M, why would you send me this?! Let's try one more tiny sip... **[45:18]** I'm retching, my eyes are watering — it tastes like charcoal, vinegar, and a mouldy Sunday dinner. I'm done! Send whaskey next time, Johnny! (**1 / 10**)

**[52:27]** Thank goodness it's time to wash that away with the world-famous whaskey review! Tonight we've got **Tomatin 18 Year Old Oloroso Sherry Cask Highland Single Malt Scotch Whisky (46% ABV)**, sent in by Nick Ball (*That Team With The Daft Hat*). A full-size bottle of this 18-year-old costs around £87.

**[54:01]** Let's get the nose in while the rain patters on the tarp above the hole:
* **Nose:** **Dried fruits**, **apricots**, and a definite **raisin** note going on, plus a nostalgic sweet putty aroma (like **Play-Doh** when you were a kid) and a **big Oloroso sherry** snifferooni!

**[55:19]** That's enough of the sniffing, let's get on with the tasting:
* **Palate:** Oh God, that's good! Fruity — **ripe dark fruits**, **berries**, and an unexpected **ripe banana** note! Very rich, very smooth, and so delicate: **zero burning sensation on the tongue, yet it still delivers a deep, glowing warmth**. That's the sign of an 18-year-old quality single malt shining right through.
* **Finish:** Tastes like a top-class single malt, a lovely drop of **Oloroso sherry**, and a rich **red wine** all at the exact same time, finishing with **tangy treacle toffee**!

**[01:00:14]** Spank you very much to Nick Ball — **Tomatin 18 Year Old Oloroso Sherry Cask** gets a **solid 9.3 out of 10**!
