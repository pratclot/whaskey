## Tasting Transcript Excerpt

**[41:29]** Three separate local lads on the streets of North Shields recommended **Miami Vice Pizza**, so I went in and ordered their **£16.50 Mega Box ('Megasaurus Box')** with a **10-inch Miami Special Pizza**, **Grilled Chicken Tikka**, **Lamb Tikka**, **Donner Meat**, **Chips**, and tubs of **Chili Sauce** and **Garlic Sauce** (plus a can of Pepsi), then hopped back onto the Shields Ferry across the River Tyne to eat it on the South Shields side!

**[48:14]** First up, the **Chips, Thick Chili Sauce & Garlic Sauce**: Both the chili and garlic sauces are proper thick (not watery at all), and the more you eat of that chili sauce, the spicier it gets — leaves a proper tingle on the tongue and makes your nose go numb!

**[49:40]** **Donner Meat**:Pulled from underneath where it stayed warm during the 15-minute ferry crossing. Tastes decent when paired with a forkful of chips and thick garlic or chili sauce.

**[52:27]** **10-Inch Miami Special Pizza**: Topped with pepperoni, ham, peppers, onions, mushrooms, garlic, and chili on a medium crust (neither too thin nor too thick). Still warm after the ferry ride, plenty of cheese and toppings, and surprisingly good quality for a £16.50 mega deal!

**[56:45]** **Grilled Chicken Tikka & Lamb Tikka**: The **Chicken Tikka** is the standout winner of the whole box — lovely and soft, marinated in tikka spice with a proper smoky barbecue char. The **Lamb Tikka** is slightly chewier than the chicken, but still tasty.

**[59:34]** At **£16.50**, the portion size is enormous (easily enough for three people — I gave the rest of the box to a local lad in South Shields town centre so nothing went to waste). Great value and solid quality: **Miami Vice Pizza gets a 7.7 out of 10!**
