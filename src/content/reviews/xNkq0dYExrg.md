## Tasting Transcript Excerpt

**[44:06]** We have got two jacket potatoes from **Spud Bros Express** (Houghton-le-Spring): **The Godfather** (minced beef chili, sour cream, loads of jalapeños and chillies) and the **Garlic Chicken** one.

**[45:20]** First up is the **Garlic Chicken Jacket Potato**. Didn't realize it was going to come with so many chillies and jalapeños on! Cheese on as well, onions, and their signature **Tram Sauce**. Piping hot — that's really nice, like. Very nice, very spicy, but very tasty at the exact same time. Better than I thought it was going to be! Both of these meals were £10.50 each, and there's plenty there — a nice big potato, loads of filling.

**[50:17]** Next one is called **The Godfather**. Straight away, that spicy chili tastes nice. It's got their signature Tram Sauce on and loads of chillies and jalapeños. That one's spicy as well! I'm trying to work out what that Tram Sauce is — it's not really spicy, it's sweet and tangy, it's got a flavour of its own not really like any other sauce I've tasted before.

**[54:33]** I tried two different ones and they've both been nice, though the **Garlic Chicken** one was better in my personal opinion. Value for money: £21 for the two, and they're certainly not greedy with the toppings — one meal would easily do.

**[55:51]** What score am I going to give? I had it in my head before I came out today I would probably just give it a 6, maybe a 7 at the most. But I have enjoyed it. I'm going to give it a **solid 8 out of 10**!
