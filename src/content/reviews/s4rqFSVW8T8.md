**[21:30]** What have we got next, guys? **Jameson Irish Whiskey with Ginger Ale and Lime** in a can. Oh, that is nice — not a bad little drop! That's a nice change, though I do prefer to drink my whaskey neat.

**[40:28]** Ordered myself a **12-inch garlic bread with cheese, a portion of chips, mayonnaise, and a tub of chili sauce** from the pizza shop over the road to make chip sarnies with the cheesy garlic bread. Piping hot — oh, that chili sauce is spicy, that's got a right kick to it! Garlic bread with cheese, chips, and chili: it's a thing of beauty.

**[53:12]** The whaskey we are going to have tonight is **Bank Hall British Single Malt Whisky** (made in Blackpool, blending English and Welsh whiskies), sent in by Nick Ball (*That Team With The Daft Hat*).

**[57:34]** It's whaskey time! I've left my whaskey cup inside my bag and I'm already zipped into my bivvy bag behind the parcel locker, so we're tasting straight from the miniature bottle. Let's get the nose in:
* **Nose:** Sweet aromas of **vanilla**, and quite a fruity element — **orange and pears**! I'm smelling pears as well as orange, and a little bit of **oak**.

**[59:36]** That's enough of the sniffing, let's get on with the tasting:
* **Palate:** Now there's no burning sensation there, guys. That is quite mild and quite delicate at the exact same time. Quite fruity — a mixture of orange and pears on the taste as well. There's no harsh spice and no burn: **oak, toffee, and a little bit of cinnamon** (Jerry Cinnamon is back in town!).

**[01:01:13]** It's a nice, easy-sipping whaskey. A full-sized bottle will set you back about £24–£25 (or £20 on sale). I would say it's not the best one for a freezing cold night when you want a heavy chest-warming kick, because I'm not getting any burn going down the chest — **but** it's fresh, crisp, light, and fruity with the pears, orange, and cinnamon. If you've tried whaskey before and thought it was too harsh, this would be a fantastic **starter whaskey** to get into.

**[01:04:22]** The last time Sunderland won a trophy was in '73 when we won the FA Cup against Leeds United — so I'm going to give **Bank Hall British Single Malt** a **7.3 out of 10**!
