## Tasting Transcript Excerpt

**[17:37]** Stopped into my favourite shop in the whole of the Metrocentre — **The Whisky Shop** — and treated myself to an £8 miniature of **Lagg Single Malt Scotch Whisky (Sherry Cask Finish, 55% ABV)** to review on tomorrow night's Newcastle stealth camp!

**[28:37]** Time for the food review at **TGI Fridays** in the Gateshead Metrocentre. I've ordered the new **Loaded Cheese Fry 'Big Ass' Burger** with skin-on fries (£24.50, plus £4.30 for a glass of Pepsi — nearly £29 altogether).

**[30:07]** First impression when the plate arrives: *"Big Ass"*? I don't think so! It's served on a kiddie-sized plate with a single beef patty inside a brioche bun topped with cheesy fries, bacon, and salad, alongside standard lightly seasoned skin-on fries.

**[32:26]** Let's get stuck into the burger: It's tasty and the cheesy fries on top are nice, **but** I've got to tell you the truth — it's no better than a burger at Wetherspoons for more than double the price! If you're going to charge £24.50 and call it a *"Big Ass Burger"*, it should be a double or triple patty, not a single burger on a kid's plate.

**[35:43]** Value for money? No chance. I'm leaving here still hungry and heading straight to **Greggs** for a black coffee and a **Steak Bake** to actually fill the hole! Because the burger itself tasted fine, I'll be generous and give **TGI Fridays** a **5 out of 10**.
