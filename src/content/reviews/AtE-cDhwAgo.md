**[01:00:05]** Let's get the nose in while the rain pours down by the lake:
* **Nose:** **Buttery toffee!** A sweet, buttery toffee snifferooni right up the nose, plus **cinnamon** (Jerry Cinnamon is back in town!).
* **Palate:** Good old warmth going down smooth, and **much thicker** than your average whaskey — thick, smooth, and delicate, followed by a wave of **mixed Christmas spices** that gives a lovely tingle on the tongue and warmth down the chest.
* **Finish:** A spicy, **zesty tangerine and orange peel** note combined with **sweet toffee, honey, and cinnamon**. With Christmas decorations starting to go up in the shops, this gives you a proper taste of Christmas!

**[01:05:30]** I thought this was going to be an expensive bottle, but you can get **Glayva** in supermarkets for around £20–£23 (or under £20 on offer). For a bargain whisky liqueur, that is top quality — **I'm giving it a solid 8.2 out of 10!**
