## Tasting Transcript Excerpt

**[27:51]** Struck gold! Proper old-fashioned style little cafe at Stanhope Train Station called **Platform One**. I ordered **Carrot & Onion Soup** with stottie bread, a **Bacon & Sausage Sarnie**, **Hot Corned Beef & Potato Pie** with coleslaw and salad, and a black coffee (£22 altogether).

**[29:33]** First up, the **Carrot & Onion Soup**: Oh, that's proper homemade soup, that! That is lovely — the hottest soup I've ever had, piping hot, warming me right up on a freezing day.

**[31:42]** Next, time for the **Bacon & Sausage Sarnie**: Bacon's nice and well done, crispy bacon and tasty sausages with a little bit of ketchup. Good quality bacon and sausages — not cheap and nasty like what you get from some cafes.

**[34:57]** Now time for the **Corned Beef & Potato Pie**: Lovely! There's a nice bit of pepper in there as well, and nice chunks of potato. Tastes homemade and piping hot — that is absolutely beautiful. The bacon and sausage sandwich was very, very nice, and the soup was lovely, but the **corned beef pie is the winner**!

**[37:32]** £22 altogether in **Platform One** at Stanhope Train Station, and the service from the ladies was fantastic. **It's a 10 out of 10 from this mandingo!**
