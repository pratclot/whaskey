## Tasting Transcript Excerpt

**[34:50]** Carl down at Birtley Town Football Club recommended **Casa Rosa** in Birtley town centre, so I've ordered a **10-inch Thin-Crust Meat Feast Pizza** and a **Chicorni Weaver Chicken Burger** (£19.35 altogether) to review back inside the Mrs. Blot Mobile.

**[38:14]** First up, the **Meat Feast Pizza** from Casa Rosa: Smells absolutely lovely, and straight away you can tell it's slightly different to what you get from a typical cheap pizza shop — you can taste the quality of the ingredients. That thin-crust pizza base has a lovely little crunch when you bite into it, with good-quality **salami, pepperoni, and ham**. My only minor gripe is that for something called a *Meat Feast*, you'd expect a bit more variety of meat piled on (like chicken or spicy beef bolognese), and at £19.35 for the order it's not a bargain meal deal, **but** the quality of the base and meats is bloody lovely.

**[44:12]** Next up, the **Chicorni Weaver Chicken Burger**: Served in a nice toasted brioche bun with spicy mayo, crisp fresh salad, pickles, and a proper breaded chicken breast fillet (thankfully not a cheap Birds Eye-style patty). The salad and pickles are lovely and fresh, and the chicken fillet tastes like a good KFC boneless fillet — tasty and fresh, even if it didn't completely blow my world away.

**[49:36]** What score am I going to give **Casa Rosa**? It's good quality and I've enjoyed it — **it's a solid 8 out of 10 from this mandingo!**
