## Tasting Transcript Excerpt

**[15:11]** I ordered two takeaway meals from **The Country Tea Shop** in Staindrop (£22.50 total): **Steak Pie with gravy, new potatoes, and veg**, and **Corned Beef & Potato Pie with chips and beans**, plus a can of Pepsi Max — though I've just looked in the bag and she hasn't given me my can of pop!

**[17:18]** First plate: **Homemade Corned Beef Pie with chips and beans**. The chips are basic frozen French fries — I was hoping for homemade chunky chips from a village tea room — **but** the homemade corned beef pie makes up for it! Piping hot, the pastry is spot on, and the corned beef is really tasty.

**[22:10]** Second plate: **Homemade Steak Pie with new potatoes, peas, proper carrots, cabbage, and gravy**. Look at that — still piping hot, lovely chunks of steak in there, and the gravy is nice and rich. Just like a lovely homemade steak pie that your mam or your nana would make. Proper good home-cooked food, and they use proper carrots rather than tinned ones.

**[29:16]** Both pies have been absolutely lovely jubbly. I think a **9 out of 10** would have been a fair score for this scran dingo, guys, but I'm going to knock half a point off for the missing £2.50 can of pop — so altogether I'm giving it an **8.5 out of 10**!
